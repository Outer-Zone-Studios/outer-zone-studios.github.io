Bubbles & Tunnels: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Bubbles & Tunnels (“the game”) is made by Oliver Goretzki, Chandler, Arizona, USA (“I”, “me”), publishing as Outer Zone Studios. This policy explains what data the game handles and why.

What the game itself stores

The game saves your best score and height, whether you have seen the how-to-play card, whether you bought “Remove ads”, and how many times you have opened and played the game (so new players are not shown ads at first). This is stored only on your device. I do not run any servers and the game sends none of this to me. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. To show, measure and protect ads, the AdMob SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device and the game (model, OS version, language),
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps),
  • crash and performance diagnostics of the ad SDK.

Google’s use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google’s privacy policy, policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the title screen.
  • In Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads) you can reset or delete your advertising ID, which stops personalised ads on your device.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional: you only see one if you choose to.

Your US state privacy rights

The game itself does not sell your personal information. Google AdMob’s use of your advertising ID to show personalised ads may count as “sharing” for targeted advertising under some US state laws, such as California’s. You can opt out with the Privacy button on the title screen where it is shown, or by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). For any other privacy request, write to the contact address below.

Purchases

If you buy “Remove ads”, the payment is handled by Google Play. I receive no payment card details; Google tells the game only that the purchase was made, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase on a new device. See Google Play’s terms and Google’s privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Data deletion

The game does not create accounts, and the data it stores stays on your device (see above). For data collected by Google for advertising, use the controls above or Google’s tools at myaccount.google.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
